# Responsible Disclosure

ZClassic accepts security reports privately and prefers coordinated disclosure: the reporter and the maintainers agree on a date for public release, after a fixed version is available, and the reporter is credited if they wish.

To report a vulnerability, use GitHub private vulnerability reporting for this repository: <https://github.com/ZclassicCommunity/zclassic/security/advisories/new>. Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability. The full process, what to include and what to expect are in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The disclosure page inherited from the Electric Coin Company (copyright The Electric Coin Company, 2019), with its Zcash contacts and agreements, is kept unchanged for reference in [doc/history/upstream-responsible-disclosure.md](doc/history/upstream-responsible-disclosure.md). It is not the ZClassic contact.
